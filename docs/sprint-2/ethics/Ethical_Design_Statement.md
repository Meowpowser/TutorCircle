# Ethical Design Statement 

## Privacy - Bassem 

TutorCircle only lets verified AUBH accounts in, and each role (Student, Tutor, Admin) can reach only the features allowed for it. The data we ask for is limited to what the features need: a student's courses, confidence levels and what they need help with, and a tutor's subjects, experience and available times. Group information (members, student ID, capacity, location, meeting time) is only visible to logged-in users and is required to enable students to schedule study sessions. Group chats are private. They are not public and can only be opened by group members and authorized admins. Support tickets and reports are sent to admins who have to deal with them in an appropriate manner. 

## Fairness - Helena 

TutorCircle does not employ a complicated matching system. Students find groups by filtering on course and availability, and the Student Needs Survey collects their course, confidence level and the topics they need help with. Only this information is used to match students to groups, so all students with the same needs will be matched to the same groups. The system ensures that there are no unfair outcomes by limiting capacity and preventing a student from being in the same group twice. All tutors have to go through the same approval process and are shown to students only after the admin has reviewed and approved their application. Every user is helped to understand what has occurred, including when an action has failed, by clear labels and error messages. 

## Safety - Maryam 

TutorCircle does not have an emergency feature, but it does emphasize prevention and review. Approved tutors are only those who have been approved by the administration and verified AUBH accounts are identifiable. Admins can view reported issues and respond to inappropriate activity and can manage user accounts and groups to resolve issues. Students have the ability to create a support ticket to report issues or seek assistance and track its progress. Group chats remain private for group members and authorized admins. If something goes wrong, the system tells the user what the issue is and doesn't give a false success message, so the user never gets confused about what went wrong. 

## Ethical Trade-Off – Fatema and Elias 

Group details display the group members, student IDs, location and meeting time. This is helpful because students can find out who they will be studying with, where and when to meet without asking around. It also implies that students' personal information is accessible to other users, which is a privacy drawback. We accept this compromise because the information is only accessible to verified AUBH users, the group chat is restricted to users and authorized admins, and admins can view any reported issue. The advantage is that it is easier to coordinate, and the disadvantage is that a student's information is available to others in the community before they choose to participate.
